our story

Why explore cambodia exists

Explore Cambodia started with a simple frustration: too many tours treat this country like a checklist — in and out of Angkor Wat, a boxed lunch, back on the bus. We wanted to build something slower, run by people who actually grew up here, that gives travelers room to feel a place instead of just photographing it. We're a small, Cambodia-based team of guides and planners working across Siem Reap, Kampot, and Mondulkiri. Every itinerary on this site is one we'd genuinely take ourselves — paced to leave room for a lingering sunset, a slow lunch, or an extra hour at the one temple that got under your skin.

our mission

to show travellers the cambodia we know - not a rushed version of it.

travel tips

before you go

01

Sort your visa in advance

Most nationalities need a visa. The e-Visa costs $30 for a single-entry, 30-day tourist stay, and is the easiest option to arrange before you fly. Air travelers must also submit a separate, free e-Arrival form within 7 days of landing — check the current process before you travel, as rules do change.

02

Dress modestly at temples

Shoulders and knees covered is the general rule at Angkor and any active pagoda. Shoes come off before entering most temple buildings

03

Carry small USD bills

Markets, tuk-tuks, and small shops often can't break large notes. $1s, $5s, and $10s go a long way.

04

Mind Buddhist etiquette

Never touch anyone's head, and avoid pointing your feet at people or Buddha images. Women shouldn't hand things directly to monks.

05

Negotiate tuk-tuk fares upfront

Agree on a price before you get in. For a full day of sightseeing, a set day-rate is usually more economical than paying trip by trip.

06

Pace yourself in the heat

Even in the cooler dry season, midday sun at the temples is intense. Water, sunscreen, and an early start make a real difference.
